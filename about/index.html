About this site

The companion to Mathematical and Computational Methods, a Level 4 module at University of Hertfordshire.

What is here

Ten units of lecture notes with 56 definitions, 144 worked examples and 54 figures, plus 474 practice problems with progressive hints and answer checking. Everything is generated from the same LaTeX source as the printed notes, so the site and the PDFs cannot drift apart.

Worked solutions

Full worked solutions are not published here. They are worked through in the tutorials, which is where the learning in them actually happens. What the site gives you instead is a ladder of hints and a way to check a final answer, so you can tell whether you have it right without being handed the method.

Your progress

Ticked objectives are stored in your own browser and nowhere else. There is no account, no login, and nothing is sent anywhere — which also means your progress does not follow you to another device, and clearing your browser data clears it.

Found a mistake?

Please report it — corrections during the first run of a module are genuinely valuable. Bring it to a lecture or tutorial, and it will be fixed at source and republished.

Colophon

Built as a static site from LaTeX: Pandoc preserves the notes' own definition, theorem and example structure, KaTeX renders the mathematics at build time, and every figure is the same TikZ drawing as in the PDF, compiled to SVG. No tracking, no cookies, no third-party requests.
