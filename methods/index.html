Methods

How to solve the standard problems of Units 1–10, in four parts. Each method gives the steps, one worked example and the mistakes to avoid.

The whole guide (PDF) All practice

Most problems on the exercise sheets are instances of a few standard types, and each type has a method: a fixed sequence of steps, taken from the notes, that solves every problem of that type. This guide collects them, unit by unit. Each method states the problem it solves, lists its steps and works one example that is not on any sheet. It ends with the mistakes to avoid, where the notes teach it, and a link to a Review set of problems of that kind. The units fall into four parts: foundations and complex numbers, vectors and matrices, differentiation, and integration.

The guide does not say which sheet problem needs which method. Recognising the type is part of what the sheets train, and in an exam nothing tells you. Where a unit’s problems look alike but need different methods, its chapter opens with a table for choosing.
